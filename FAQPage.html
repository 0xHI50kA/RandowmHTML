Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of this website?

This website is designed to provide users with information about our products and services, as well as to answer common questions.

How do I track my order?

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your order on our website or the carrier's website.

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unused items in their original packaging. Please contact our customer support team to initiate a return.

Why don't I have Internet?

Please check your network connection and ensure your router is powered on. If the issue persists, contact your Internet service provider.
